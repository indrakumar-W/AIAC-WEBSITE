Applied Research & Systems Engineering

Advancing Applied Intelligence and Scalable Computing Systems.

The Advanced Intelligence & Applied Computing (AIAC) initiative operates at the intersection of production systems, multi-agent frameworks, and compute-efficient model optimization.

01 / Mission

AIAC structures research like modern engineering organizations: short sprint cadences, modular architecture, and public validation through code and open publications.

We incubate early-stage engineers through direct collaboration with senior industry architects and applied AI practitioners, focusing on real-world constraints over purely theoretical models.

02 / Research Domains

Three core areas of applied investigation and systems engineering.

Agentic Systems

Multi-agent consensus protocols, deterministic tool execution, and automated context retrieval workflows designed for complex enterprise pipelines.

Model Systems & Edge

Inference latency optimization, post-training quantization, and model serving across heterogeneous, resource-constrained hardware targets.

Regional NLP & Vision

Low-resource Indic language representations, structured document intelligence, and real-time visual anomaly detection on streaming feeds.

03 / Dual Mentorship

Systems Architecture Advisory

Bi-weekly strategic syncs led by a Principal Architect (15+ Years Industry Experience) focusing on system resilience, scalability, and intellectual property.

Engineering & Code Audits

Weekly standups led by an Applied AI Practitioner (3+ Years Experience in Agents & LLMs) enforcing trunk-based Git flows, PR reviews, and testing rigor.

04 / Intake

Cohort Induction

Open to undergraduate engineers in early semesters. Admission is merit-based and determined solely through a 48-hour hands-on technical screening task.
