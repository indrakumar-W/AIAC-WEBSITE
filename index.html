CSE Research Center of Excellence

Advancing Applied Computing and Intelligent Systems

The Advanced Intelligence & Applied Computing (AIAC) Lab incubates undergraduate researchers at BMSIT & M, focusing on scalable, production-grade intelligence, optimization, and human-centric computing.

Research Domains Meet the Team

Research Focus

Defining the Future of Applied Intelligence

Cognitive & Agentic Systems

Research into adaptive multi-agent orchestration, resilient decision-making frameworks, and contextual retrieval mechanisms for complex, open-ended environments.

Scalable Autonomy • Multi-Agent consensus • Planning Workflows

Model Systems & Optimization

Optimizing computational inference, establishing robust deployment architectures, and defining production standards for large-scale applied models across diverse hardware.

Model Quantization • Serving Architectures • Performance Benchmarking

Spatial Intelligence & Vision

Advancing visual reasoning and spatial understanding, including efficient real-time anomaly detection, semantic analysis, and human-environment interaction.

Visual Reasoning • Real-time Anomaly Detection • Semantic Interpretation
